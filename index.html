Welfare Contribution Program

Our welfare program is designed to encourage members to support one another when genuine needs arise.

1. Member Contribution

Members participate according to the association's approved welfare contribution arrangements.

2. Welfare Request

A member can submit a welfare request when support is needed.

3. Review

Authorized association representatives review requests and available welfare resources.

4. Support

Approved support is provided according to the association's rules and available funds.

All welfare contributions and distributions should follow the association's approved rules and accountability procedures.
